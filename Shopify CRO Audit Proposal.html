Where it usually happens

Three places revenue quietly disappears.

Every store leaks differently, but in fifteen years of auditing Shopify funnels, the drop-off almost always clusters around one of three stages.

Stage 01 — Product Page

The case isn't made

  • Weak or generic product messaging
  • Mobile layout slows the scroll
  • No real trust signals near the buy button
  • Shipping & return info buried or missing
Stage 02 — Cart

Second thoughts creep in

  • Unexpected costs surface late
  • No urgency or reassurance present
  • Upsells distract instead of support the decision
  • Cart feels disconnected from the brand
Stage 03 — Checkout

Friction at the finish line

  • Too many steps, too many fields
  • Limited or unclear payment options
  • Tax & shipping costs feel confusing
  • No reassurance at the exact moment of doubt
